Paddlemate · direction 3, calm modern

Calm modern, across the app

Soft white surfaces on a cool grey ground, one friendly typeface, gentle depth, rounded shapes and glacier teal as the one accent. The data carries the colour; the chrome stays quiet. Every screen uses the dev fixture and the app's own words.

Surfaces, not linesLists are cards on a ground; the panel header is a white sheet with a soft shadow. Rules appear only inside a card.
One typefaceFigtree, mixed case everywhere, tabular figures for numbers. No capitals, no mono.
Glacier teal as the accentTabs, selection, the main button. Lime retires; selection is a teal ring. Rivers on the map stay a pale blue.
Levels as pillsDot plus reading. Too low is an outline, low and medium washed, high solid, so the level reads without colour.
Rounded, on a scale6 for badges, 10 for controls, 14 for cards, 16 for panels and the button, full round for pills and dots.
Depth in three stepsCards rest, popovers float, the main button lifts. Nothing else casts a shadow.

Made for touch

Every target fits a thumb

Measured on the running app at phone size, many controls are well below the 44px that Apple and WCAG recommend. The design sets a floor instead of judging by eye: in the screens on this page, all 241 tappable elements now measure 44px or more, and every text field uses 16px text so iPhones do not zoom when it is tapped.

44px minimum, 48 for rows and tabsSmall icons keep their size; the area around them is what you tap.
8px between targetsTwo targets never touch unless they are one segmented control.
16px text in every fieldBelow that, iOS zooms the page on focus. Times and dates use the phone's own pickers.
Actions in thumb reachThe main action is the bottom bar or the FAB; anything that removes sits away from it and always asks first.
Nothing only on hoverTips like "Lock area" or the gauge count's explanation get a visible label or open on a tap.
Clear of the system barsBottom bars and the FAB add the phone's safe area, so the home indicator never covers them.

Map, region mode

Regions on the map

Today. Dashed outlines in eight colours, shared edges drawn twice, "AT ·" in front of every name, white labels on a busy map.

Browsing. One thin grey line per edge, one name per range in letter-spaced grey capitals, towns stay mixed case. The base map is muted. A range that holds two or more others in view is not drawn, so none overlaps another. The chip row sets the level.

Overlap. A tap picks the innermost region at once. The others wait as radio rows under "2 regions here", and the one you are not choosing keeps a firm grey outline.

Picked. The name and the clear button take the search field's place; the level chips go away. The sheet is a peek, so the map keeps most of the screen. Drag up for the full list.

Open sheet. "Part of" chips widen the pick one step (Bezirk Imst, Ötztaler Alpen), then Rivers and Sections. Search and chips are back while the sheet is open.

Valleys. A valley is a soft band with its name running along it, and the full outline in teal only when picked. Tiny gullies wait for a closer zoom.

320px. The same peek on the smallest phone: the map runs down to about 410px of 568. Before the peek, a pick left only 126px of it.

What this direction changes in the rules

Rounded corners

The theme is square by rule today. This direction needs the radius scale above, which rewrites that rule in AGENTS.md and doc/design.md.

Shadows

"No elevation shadows" gives way to three depth steps: card, popover, main button.

Light first

Drawn light by default with a dark version. The app is dark-only today; both together follow the system setting.

Selection

Lime goes; selection becomes a teal ring, and the main button turns glacier teal.
